For who it’s for

Made for your moment

is for you if

  • You coordinate a team and processes and you want ready-to-use material, without rewriting everything.
  • You prepare lessons and follow-ups and you want to save time without losing your way of writing.
  • You’ve built your career and you want AI to fit into how you work.
  • You use an AI chat from time to time and you want to stop improvising with every request.
  • You have little time and you’d rather understand through the drawing.

maybe not now

  • You want to practice within the lesson, with a long step-by-step. The full edition works best.
  • You’re looking for quick-answer tricks, without setting up a fixed place for your work.
  • You want to study how the technology works from the inside. Here we organize how you use it.

What is missing

Nothing here asks for technical knowledge

you won’t find

  • Programming. You won’t write a single line of code in any lesson.
  • Black screen of commands. Everything happens on the screens you already use.
  • Installing programs. Nothing needs to be downloaded to your computer.

you will find

  • Drawings that show the mechanism before any explanation.
  • Examples of a manager and a teacher, in every lesson.
  • A short step at the end of each lesson, to do with your material.
